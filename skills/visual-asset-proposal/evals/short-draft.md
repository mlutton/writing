# Put the label where the lid won't take it

I used to write the name of a leftover dish on its lid. I'd put the box
in the fridge and move on. Later somebody would lift the lid to take a
portion, swap it with a lid that fit, or put it down next to the sink.
Then I'd have a container with no label and a lid that said “lentils”
sitting next to an empty bowl.

The fix was to put the label on the side of the container. I picked one
side that I could see from the front of the fridge. If the container
went behind something taller, I could still pull it out partway and read
the label without taking the whole stack apart.

I started writing the date in the corner of the same label. That comes
in handy when there are two containers of the same soup and I can't
remember which one we opened first. I don't need a different system for
that. I just need room to write the date next to the name.

A strip of tape works for most containers. I wipe the surface before
putting the tape on so it doesn't slide off when the box is damp. Some
containers have a ribbed side that makes tape hard to read. On those I
use the flat patch near the bottom and turn it toward the door.

The change didn't make our fridge neater. It did make it easier to answer
a small question when I was hungry and in a hurry. The next time I reached
for a box, I could take the lid off, put it down, and still know what I'd
picked up.
