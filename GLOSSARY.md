These terms describe the stages of writing and the material used along the way.

## Language

### Topic

A subject or idea proposed for an article.

_Avoid_: pitch, ticket

### Draft

The working text of an article in progress.

_Avoid_: manuscript, rough copy

### Article

A piece of writing intended for publication.

_Avoid_: content asset, deliverable

### Question round

A set of questions for the author to settle decisions about an article.

_Avoid_: intake, interrogation

### Persona

A fictional reviewer profile that provides a consistent reading perspective.

_Avoid_: character, avatar

### Persona round

A set of persona reviews of the same draft.

_Avoid_: focus group, panel

### Review hash

A fingerprint that identifies the exact draft text reviewed in a round.

_Avoid_: version label, revision number

### Guide

A reference that records the author’s preferences for writing or presentation.

_Avoid_: playbook, recipe

### Published entry

A short summary of a published article with its publication date and link.

_Avoid_: mirror, archive copy

### Public repo

The repository for shared writing tools and published entries.

_Avoid_: public workspace, publication archive

### Private repo

The repository for working material, personal references, and complete article text.

_Avoid_: scratch space, local cache

### Guard

A check for material that should stay out of the public repo.

_Avoid_: linter, formatter
